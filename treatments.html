Before you book

Will you tell me what the remedy is?

Yes. You will know what you are taking, how to take it and what changes to look for.

How soon will I see a change?

It varies. Recent, acute problems may respond within days. Long-standing conditions usually need weeks to months and regular review.

When should I see another doctor instead?

For emergencies, severe pain, high fever, breathing difficulty or sudden changes, go to your nearest hospital first. We can continue supportive care afterwards.

Not sure where your problem fits?

Describe it on the appointment form. The clinic will tell you if it is something we can help with.
