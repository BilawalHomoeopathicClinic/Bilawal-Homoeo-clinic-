A clinic built around conversation.

We believe patients get better when they are properly understood.

Our consultations are slow on purpose. We want to know how you sleep, what you eat, what worries you and when your symptoms are worse, because that is how a remedy is matched to a person.

  • Individualised prescriptions, never one remedy for everyone with the same diagnosis.
  • Plain explanations of what the remedy is, what to expect and when to review.
  • Honest advice, including when you need a specialist or urgent care.
