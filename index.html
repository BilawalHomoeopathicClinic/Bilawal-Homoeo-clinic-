Questions patients ask

How long does the first consultation take?

Plan for a longer first visit than usual, because the doctor needs your full history. Follow-up visits are shorter.

Are the remedies safe for children and older adults?

Homoeopathic remedies are highly diluted and are commonly used across all ages. The doctor will adjust the plan to the patient and tell you what to expect.

Can I continue my regular medicines?

Please never stop prescribed medicines on your own. Tell the doctor everything you take so the plan works alongside your existing care.

How do the discounts and free camps work?

Offers are listed on the Offers page with who they are for and until when. Choose the offer when you book, or show the required ID at the clinic.
