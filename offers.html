Offers and free camps.

Reduced fees for particular patient groups, and free camps in the community. This page updates as new offers are announced.

How to claim an offer

It takes a minute, and there is nothing to pay in advance.

  1. Choose your offer

    Press the button on the offer card, or select it in the offer menu on the appointment form.

  2. Send your request

    Send it by WhatsApp or email with your details filled in. The clinic confirms your slot.

  3. Bring proof if needed

    Some offers need an ID, such as a student card or proof of age. It is noted on the offer.
