Offer manager

How this works
  1. Add, edit or delete offers below.
  2. Press Download offers.js.
  3. Replace the file js/offers.js in the website folder with the downloaded one and upload the site again.
Expired offers disappear from the website by themselves after their end date.

Your offers

Add an offer

Save your picture as a JPG, about 1600 px wide, in the img/offers folder of the website, and type the same name here. Put the people or the main subject on the right side, because the offer text sits on the left.
